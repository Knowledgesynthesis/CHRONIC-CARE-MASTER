# **6. Chronic Disease Reasoning Framework**

Universal logic:
1. Determine disease **classification** (severity/stage).  
2. Evaluate **control level** using symptom trackers + synthetic labs.  
3. Review **trend data** (A1c, BP, EFs, symptoms).  
4. Decide on **step-up vs step-down**.  
5. Identify **complications or red flags**.  
6. Integrate comorbidities.  
7. Establish **monitoring interval** (conceptual).  
8. Plan **long-term follow-up** and lifestyle anchors.

**Pitfalls to emphasize:**
- Over-relying on single A1c/BP readings  
- Confusing HFrEF vs HFpEF  
- Not adjusting asthma therapy based on control  
- Misinterpreting COPD symptom burden  
- Failing to differentiate depression from burnout or medical causes  
- Missing cardiac or pulmonary contributors to anxiety or fatigue
